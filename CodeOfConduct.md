# Team Code of Conduct

## 1. Core Principles

Mutual Respect: Value every team member's ideas and constructive feedback.

Shared Ownership: The entire team owns the final product, regardless of individual task distributions.

Transparency: Communicate progress, and changes early and openly.

## 2. Team Organization & Roles

To maintain accountability across 6 members, primary responsibilities are designated below (technical work remains shared):

Project Owner: Leads weekly syncs, maintains task status, and tracks project milestones.

Scrum Master: Oversees pull requests, branch protection rules, and merge conflicts.

Team Members (All 6): Actively write, review, and test code, participate in discussions, and adhere to agreed deadlines.

## 3. Communication Standards

Primary Channels

Main Chat (Telegram): For day-to-day coordination, quick questions, and quick status updates.

GitHub Issues / Project Board: For task tracking, feature requests, and bug reports.

Expectations
Response Time: Respond to direct team tags within 24 hours on weekdays and 36 hours on weekends.

Absences: If an emergency or heavy workload prevents you from working or attending a meeting, notify the group in advance via the primary chat.

**Failure to respond within designated hours without advanced notification will result in deduction in marks**

## 4. Meetings & Collaboration

Weekly Sync: Hold a 30-minute sync meeting weekly to review progress, set goals, and reassign stalled tasks.

Agendas: Meetings must have a short, predetermined agenda created by the Scrum Master.

Punctuality: Arrive on time. If you are going to be late, inform the team before the start time.

## 5. Development & Git Workflow

Branch Strategy: Never commit directly to main or dev. Create feature or bugfix branches (feature/feature-name or fix/bug-name).

Pull Requests (PRs):

Every PR requires peer approval before merging into dev.

Keep PRs small and focused on a single task or issue.

Authors must ensure code builds and unit tests pass before requesting a review.

## 6. Academic Integrity & Quality Standards

Originality: All submitted code must be written by team members or properly credited according to course guidelines.

**Do not plagiarize or submit work which is not your own.**

## 7. Conflict Resolution & Accountability

Direct Discussion: Address minor disagreements or miscommunications directly and calmly in private or during the weekly sync.

Group Review: If an issue persists (e.g., missed deadlines or incomplete tasks), convene a full team discussion to reassign tasks or adjust project scope.

Escalation: If a team member remains unresponsive or uncooperative for more than 3 consecutive days, the team will hold a meeting with module professor to discuss and make decision on mark reduction.

## 8. Rules Violation and Marks

Any violation of rules will result in mark deduction of **0.5** for each time.

If there exists any major violation or consecutive violations of rules, the aftermath punishment of such violation will be discussed with team members (or) module professor.

## 9. Compliance

Non-compliance with these standards may cause you to be removed from the team and fail the coursework under official university rules.
